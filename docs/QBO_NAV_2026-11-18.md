# QuickBooks Online navigation: what changed by 2026-11-18

The company menu map in `public/apps/ledger/qb-navigation.json` was captured on
2026-10-26. This note records a fresh observation of the same product on 2026-11-18, what
drifted, and what was added to the map as a result.

Evidence: [company navigation labels](evidence/qbo-company-nav-2026-11-18.json). Labels
only — no client name, amount or period is stored.

## What the current navigation shows

The rail now renders firm and company items together:

`My menu · Create · Bookmarks · Home · Clients · Work · Firm hub · Firm Books ·
Accounting · Sales · Customize`

Expanding the app menu yields, among others: **Reports & Analytics** (Standard reports,
Custom reports, Management reports, KPIs, Spreadsheet sync, Performance centre, Financial
planning, Cash flow overview, Cash flow planner, Budgets, Forecasts), **Client overview**,
**Bank transactions**, **Integration transactions**, **Receipts**, **Reconcile**, **Rules**,
**Chart of accounts**, **Recurring transactions**, **Revenue recognition**,
**Fixed assets**, **Prepaid expenses**, **Expenses & Bills**, **Sales & Get Paid**,
**Customer Hub**, **Payroll**, **Team** (Time entries, Approvals, Schedule, Time off, Team,
Assignments, Projects, Time reports), **Inventory**, **Sales Tax**, **Business Tax**,
**Workpapers**.

## Drift against the October map

| Observation | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Labels in today's navigation that the map does not contain | 19 |
| Map labels not rendered by this navigation surface | 93 |

The second number is **not** evidence of removal. The map also covers the Create menu,
Settings and the Reports submenu, and this capture was one surface. Only the first number
is drift.

The 19 new labels are: firm-scope items (Clients, Work, Firm hub, Firm Books, and the
account name), the **Reports & Analytics** grouping, the **Sales** grouping, the whole
**Time** suite, and **Business Tax**.

## What was added to the map

A new group, **"Company navigation (observed 2026-11-18)"**, with 18 entries pinned to
this capture. Two carry a destination:

- `Reports & Analytics > Standard reports` → the fixed native report set.
- Everything else → no destination, each with the reason: no time model, no scheduling,
  no leave tracking, no sales-tax filing, and firm-scope areas that belong in the Firm
  app rather than the Ledger.

The group's own scope line says firm items appear on this rail but are not Ledger screens.
That is deliberate: mapping `Clients` to a Ledger route would have been a false promise.

## Settings was restructured

`/app/accountsettings` now presents settings as **tabs**: Company, Notifications, Reports,
Payments, Accounting, Sales, Inventory, Expenses, Time, Payroll, Advanced, Edit.

The October map describes the old grouped structure — **YOUR COMPANY / LISTS / TOOLS /
PROFILE** — with 39 entries. Those four section headings no longer exist on the screen.
Rather than delete the entries, each section node now carries the restructure note in its
scope line, so the map says "captured 2026-10-26, superseded" instead of implying currency.

Individual settings behind each tab were **not** opened, so this is a restructure finding,
not a list of removed settings.

## The Reports branch is intact

The eleven report labels captured from the current navigation — Standard reports, Custom
reports, Management reports, KPIs, Spreadsheet sync, Performance centre, Financial
planning, Cash flow overview, Cash flow planner, Budgets, Forecasts — **all already exist**
in the map.

The map also holds report *names* (balance sheet, profit and loss, aging summary and so on)
that this navigation does not render. Those are not removals: the
[report catalogue](QBO_REPORT_COVERAGE.md) confirms `BAL_SHEET`, `PANDL` and `AR_AGING`
still exist as reports. Only `Dashboards` could not be re-confirmed.

## Create menu: still unverified

The Create popover did not render into the captured selectors on three attempts, from the
firm hub, from the firm client list and from company settings. The Create branch of the map
therefore remains as captured on 2026-10-26.

## Boundary

- One company, one day, one navigation surface. QBO varies by plan, country and enabled
  features; this is an observation, not the vendor's complete inventory.
- No screen behind the labels was opened, so nothing here describes behaviour inside a
  report, list or form.
- The October map remains unverified outside this surface; the earlier attempt to re-check
  it against a full company workspace failed at every automated route (see
  [the firm-hub note](QBO_FIRM_HUB.md)).
