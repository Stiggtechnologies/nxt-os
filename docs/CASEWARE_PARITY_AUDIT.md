## Required evidence to close a comparison

Record source and NXT intermediate lines, balance bases, totals/diagnostics, document version, sign-off behavior and generated artifacts. A one-cent/account/period difference must be explained; global balance alone cannot hide misposting. Document deliberate original NXT policy differences instead of declaring them matches.

Separate positive/invalid/empty/conflict/retry, permission, lock/unlock and recovery cases. Preserve source engagement/readable archives and unsupported source capabilities; no legacy cutover follows from an initial native module. Prove each import/export target contract independently. Do not load or redistribute proprietary templates, registration material or private client files to manufacture coverage.
